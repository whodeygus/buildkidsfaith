    Common Questions

    Can I share the guides with my team?

    Absolutely. One subscription covers your entire local church campus, both age tracks included. You are free to print and distribute the Teacher and Volunteer guides to your staff.

    Do you have anything for older kids?

    Yes. Every subscription includes our Student Edition, built for grades 6-12, alongside the Younger Kids track. Both follow the same monthly stories and scripture, so families with kids at different ages can talk about the same message around the dinner table.

    Which Bible version is used?

    The curriculum is designed to be version-agnostic, though primary scripture references are provided in clear, modern translations that are easy for children to grasp.

    How is the content delivered?

    Right after checkout, you'll get an email invite to set your account password. Once you log in, your curriculum is ready to download instantly from your Member Downloads page — no waiting.

    Can I cancel my subscription?

    Yes. We are here to serve your ministry, not trap you in a contract. Log in and click "Manage My Subscription" in your Member Downloads area to cancel either plan yourself, anytime, through your secure billing portal.
