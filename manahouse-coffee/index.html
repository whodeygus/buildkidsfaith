Our Story

From Service To Espresso

Manahouse Coffee was founded by a U.S. military veteran who learned that the best missions end around a table: a hot cup of coffee in hand, trading stories with the people who had your back.

We built Manahouse to be that table for Jacksboro and Campbell County. It's a place where neighbors become regulars, strangers become game-night teammates, and every cup is made with the same care and discipline learned in uniform.

In gaming, mana is the energy that powers your next move. We think a great cup of coffee does the same.
