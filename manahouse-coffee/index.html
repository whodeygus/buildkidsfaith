Our Story

Coffee Served With Purpose

Manahouse Coffee is veteran-owned and operated. It was co-founded by Donald Leatherwood (U.S. Army) and Amanda Baker (U.S. Navy), two people who traveled the country, fell in love with Campbell County, and decided to put down roots here.

What started as a dream to build community became a beloved gathering place for Jacksboro. Every syrup is handcrafted from scratch using real, natural ingredients.

We believe great coffee belongs in the middle of great community. That's why we host game nights, keep our doors open late on Fridays, and make sure everyone who walks in feels genuinely welcome.
